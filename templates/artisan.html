🔧 Artisan & Vocational Training Programs

Artisan programs are 1-year intensive practical courses in skilled trades. These programs prepare students for immediate self-employment or technical employment.

  • Duration: 1 year (2 semesters)
  • Entry: Minimum D- in KCSE - very accessible
  • Outcome: Artisan Certificate (Grade I, II, or III)
  • Career: Self-employment, technical employment, diploma upgrade

🔧 Why Choose Artisan & Vocational Training

🛠️ Immediate Self-Employment

Start your own business immediately after graduation. Artisan skills like plumbing, electrical, and carpentry are always in demand for independent contracting.

💰 Low Cost, High Return

Affordable training with minimal fees. Artisan trades offer excellent income potential with skilled workers earning KES 30,000-80,000+ monthly.

🏗️ Essential Trade Skills

Learn critical building and maintenance trades that power Kenya's construction and infrastructure sectors. Skilled artisans are in constant demand.

📈 Upgrade Pathway

Artisan certificates can be upgraded to Craft Certificates and then Diplomas. Build your qualifications progressively while earning from your trade.

Yes! D plain and D- grades qualify for all artisan programs. These courses focus on practical skills rather than academic grades. Your willingness to learn and work hard matters more than your KCSE performance.

Artisan is the entry-level (1 year, D- minimum). Craft Certificate is intermediate (1-2 years, D+ minimum). Diploma is advanced (2-3 years, C- minimum). You can progress through all levels, upgrading your skills and qualifications.

Self-employed artisans earn KES 30,000-80,000+ monthly depending on skill level and location. Employed artisans start at KES 15,000-25,000. Specialized trades like electrical and plumbing command premium rates in urban areas.

Yes, through the progressive pathway: Artisan → Craft Certificate → Diploma. Each level builds on the previous one. Many successful diploma graduates started as artisan students and worked their way up while gaining experience.

Government artisan programs cost KES 5,000-15,000 per year. Some are free in public vocational centers. Private colleges charge KES 20,000-50,000. County bursaries and NITA funding often cover costs completely.

Yes, NITA (National Industrial Training Authority) offers trade test certifications for artisans. These nationally recognized certificates validate your skills and are required for government contracts and formal employment in many trades.

KUCCPS artisan placement allocates students to government-sponsored artisan slots in vocational training centers. Applications are open to all KCSE graduates with D- and above. Placement is based on preference and availability.

📋 Artisan Program Prerequisites and Requirements

Minimum Academic Requirements
  • 📊 Overall KCSE Grade: Minimum D- (1 point) - most accessible
  • 🎓 Education Level: KCSE completion or equivalent
  • 📖 Basic Literacy: Ability to read and write
  • 👤 Age Requirement: Usually 16+ years at entry
  • ✅ Physical Ability: Some trades require manual dexterity
Program-Specific Requirements
  • 🔧 Construction Trades: Physical fitness, spatial awareness, tool handling
  • ⚡ Electrical Trades: Color vision, safety consciousness, math basics
  • 🚗 Mechanical Trades: Mechanical aptitude, problem-solving ability
  • 💇 Service Trades: Customer service skills, creativity, hygiene awareness
  • ✅ All Programs: Commitment, punctuality, and willingness to learn
