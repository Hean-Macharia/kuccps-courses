Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Does a course match guarantee KUCCPS placement or admission?

No. Courses Checker provides eligibility guidance based on the information you enter. Official placement and admission decisions are made by KUCCPS and the institutions.

What should I have ready before checking?

Have your KCSE result slip available so you can enter the overall result and the subject grades requested by your selected pathway.

How do I check KUCCPS courses using my KCSE grades?

Choose a programme category, enter your KCSE overall and subject grades, then submit the assessment. Courses Checker shows eligibility guidance based on the available course data. Confirm current requirements and apply through KUCCPS.

Are the course requirements the same for every programme?

No. Entry requirements vary by programme and institution. Review the requirements shown with each assessment and confirm current details with the institution or KUCCPS.

What does KUCCPS do?

The Kenya Universities and Colleges Central Placement Service (KUCCPS) coordinates placement of eligible students to universities, colleges and other higher-learning institutions in Kenya. Courses Checker is independent guidance; applications and official placement decisions are handled through KUCCPS and the institutions.

Courses Checker is an independent eligibility guidance tool. A match does not guarantee official KUCCPS placement or admission.
