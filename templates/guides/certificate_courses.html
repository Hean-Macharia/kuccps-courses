Certificate Courses in Kenya

Build your skills and career - Accessible programs for practical learning and employment

Guidance checked: November 2026 Read time: 10 min Category: Technical Training

Certificate programmes span many fields and institutions. Entry conditions, required subjects, duration, fees, and application routes differ by course; a general grade threshold cannot describe every certificate programme. Use the active programme listing and institution information to check the details that apply to you.

How to check certificate requirements

Search the current KUCCPS programme list if the course is offered through KUCCPS placement. Read the full programme entry, including institution, campus, required subjects, and any published grade conditions. For programmes outside a KUCCPS placement cycle, contact the institution directly.

For current programme information, use the official KUCCPS Student Portal.

Plan the next step

Ask the institution about study mode, course duration, fees, assessment, and whether progression to another qualification is available. These details depend on the specific programme and are not guaranteed by the award title alone.

Compare certificate pathways

Courses Checker compares entered KCSE grades with information in its catalogue. Treat the result as guidance only. Confirm current requirements, programme availability, and application dates with KUCCPS or the training institution.

Browse all student guides or return to the Courses Checker homepage.
