Student Funding and Scholarships in Kenya

Make higher education affordable - Complete guide to funding your studies in Kenya

Guidance checked: November 2026 Read time: 13 min Category: Financial Planning

Funding for higher education may include scholarships, loans, bursaries, or institution-specific support. Eligibility, application windows, award amounts, and repayment terms depend on the provider and can change. Treat the provider’s current notice as authoritative.

Where to verify student funding

Check the official Higher Education Financing (HEF) portal and the relevant funding provider for current eligibility, required documents, deadlines, application status, and review procedures. Do not use old articles or social posts as proof that an award is currently open.

For current funding information, use the official HEF portal and verify guidance with the named provider.

Prepare carefully and protect your information

Read the provider’s privacy and consent information before submitting personal details. Use the official portal, keep your confirmation, and be cautious of intermediaries asking for passwords, payment, or sensitive documents outside the published process.

Funding is separate from course placement

Courses Checker provides course eligibility guidance; it does not award scholarships, arrange loans, or guarantee funding. Verify programme placement with KUCCPS and funding status with the official provider.
