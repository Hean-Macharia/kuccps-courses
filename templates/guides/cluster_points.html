KUCCPS Cluster Points and Cut-Off Points Explained

Understand weighted cluster points, programme cut-offs, and where to verify your official information

Guidance checked: November 2026 Read time: 10 min Category: Admission Guides

KUCCPS publishes weighted cluster points and programme cut-off points to help applicants assess degree choices. These terms describe different things, and the official portal is the source for an applicant’s own weighted cluster points.

Weighted cluster points and cut-off points

According to the KUCCPS Frequently Asked Questions, weighted cluster points are computed using the four subjects required for a degree programme, relative to candidates’ performance in that examination year. Cut-off points are the weighted cluster points of the last competitively placed applicant in a programme.

KUCCPS advises applicants not to calculate their own weighted cluster points because the placement system uses KCSE performance data, including performance indices supplied by KNEC. Check your own figures in the official KUCCPS Student Portal.

How to check your official figures

  1. Open the KUCCPS Student Portal and sign in using the current instructions from KUCCPS.
  2. Review the programme list and downloads for the current application cycle.
  3. Check programme subject requirements and the weighted cluster points shown for your account.
  4. Compare the programme information available for the relevant cycle. Previous cut-off points are a guide and do not guarantee placement.
  5. Confirm application dates, choices, and final placement directly with KUCCPS.

Why cut-off points vary

A cut-off reflects the last applicant placed in a programme during a particular placement cycle. It can differ by programme, institution, and year as applicant performance and available places change. A previous cut-off is not a promised threshold for a future cycle.

Meeting minimum subject and grade requirements allows an applicant to consider a programme; it does not guarantee selection. Follow the current KUCCPS application guidance and use the data displayed in the official portal.

What Courses Checker can help with

Courses Checker compares entered KCSE grades with programme information available in its catalogue and presents eligibility guidance. It is an independent information tool, not the KUCCPS placement system. It does not calculate or replace KUCCPS weighted cluster points, and its results do not guarantee admission or placement.

For the current programme list and official applicant-specific cluster information, use the KUCCPS Student Portal and the official KUCCPS FAQs.
