KMTC Courses and Admission in Kenya

Train for a rewarding healthcare career with Kenya Medical Training College

Guidance checked: November 2026 Read time: 10 min Category: Health Professions

Kenya Medical Training College (KMTC) programmes and campuses are listed through official sources. Available courses, campus options, subject requirements, and application windows can change by intake. Check the current KUCCPS programme information and KMTC notices before making a decision.

How to verify a KMTC programme

Use the current official programme listing to confirm the exact course title, campus, required KCSE subjects and grades, and application instructions. Do not rely on a requirement copied from another KMTC course or an earlier intake.

Check current KUCCPS placement information on the official Student Portal and review KMTC’s academic programmes.

Prepare for an application

Follow the instructions for the active intake on the official portal. Confirm the application window, required documents, fees if applicable, and any campus-specific information directly with KUCCPS or KMTC. Courses Checker does not submit an application or confirm admission.

Compare healthcare pathways

Use Courses Checker to compare your KCSE grades with KMTC programme information available in its catalogue. The result is independent eligibility guidance and does not guarantee admission or placement.
