KCSE Grades and University Admission Requirements in Kenya

Learn how to compare your grades with current programme requirements and official placement guidance

Guidance checked: November 2026 Read time: 12 min Category: Admission Guides

KCSE results help determine which programmes an applicant may consider, but there is no single grade table that describes every course. Entry requirements can include a mean grade and specific subject grades, and they vary by programme, institution, and placement cycle.

Check the requirements for each programme

Use the current KUCCPS Student Portal programme list and downloads to review the institution, programme code, subject requirements, and any current cut-off information. A general summary on another website cannot replace the official programme entry.

Open the official KUCCPS Student Portal and confirm the information for the cycle in which you intend to apply.

How to compare your KCSE results

  1. Read the programme’s published minimum mean-grade requirement.
  2. Check every required subject and grade. A matching mean grade alone may not meet a programme’s conditions.
  3. For degree programmes, use the weighted cluster points shown in your KUCCPS portal. KUCCPS advises applicants not to calculate these themselves because the official calculation uses KNEC performance data.
  4. Check the institution and programme code carefully; similar course names can have different requirements.
  5. Confirm that the programme is available and that the current application instructions allow you to select it.

Explore different study pathways

Students explore degree, diploma, certificate, health training, artisan, and teacher-training programmes. Their requirements are not interchangeable: compare the published conditions for each specific programme rather than assuming a single grade threshold applies to an entire category.

Use eligibility tools as guidance

Courses Checker compares entered KCSE grades with programme information available in its catalogue. It is an independent guidance tool and does not make placement decisions. Always verify current requirements, programme availability, deadlines, and your applicant-specific information with KUCCPS and the institution.

Meeting stated requirements does not guarantee a place: placement depends on the official process and available programme capacity.
