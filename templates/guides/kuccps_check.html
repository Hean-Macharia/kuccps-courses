How to Check KUCCPS Courses by KCSE Grades

Complete step-by-step guide to finding and checking all available university and college programs

Guidance checked: November 2026 Read time: 8 min Category: Application Guide

To check current KUCCPS programme options, use the official Student Portal and read the instructions for the active placement cycle. Courses Checker is a separate guidance tool that compares KCSE grades with programme information available in its catalogue.

Check programmes on the official KUCCPS portal

  1. Open the KUCCPS Student Portal and follow its current sign-in instructions.
  2. Review the current programme list and downloads. KUCCPS identifies the portal’s Programmes and Downloads sections as sources for course subject requirements.
  3. Read each programme entry carefully, including institution, campus, subject requirements, programme code, and any available cut-off information.
  4. Check your applicant-specific information in the portal. KUCCPS advises applicants to use the weighted cluster points it computes rather than trying to calculate them independently.
  5. Follow the active-cycle application steps and keep the confirmation shown by the portal.

Compare course options using KCSE grades

Start with the exact course category and programme. Compare the mean-grade and subject conditions published for that programme; broad assumptions about what a student qualifies for can be misleading because requirements differ.

Courses Checker presents independent eligibility guidance based on the course information in its catalogue. It is not the KUCCPS portal, does not calculate official weighted cluster points, and cannot guarantee selection or placement.

  • University degree courses
  • Diploma courses
  • KMTC programmes
  • Certificate courses
  • Artisan pathways
  • Teacher-training pathways

Verify information before applying

Application dates, available programmes, requirements, and placement outcomes change by cycle. Confirm all current details in official KUCCPS notices and with the institution. Meeting published minimum requirements does not guarantee placement because placement is competitive and depends on available programme places.

For a deeper explanation, read weighted cluster points and cut-off points or the KCSE admission guide.
