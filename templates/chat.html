Frequently Asked Questions

What courses can I do with C plain in KCSE?

With a C plain in KCSE, you can apply for Diploma programs (minimum C-), Certificate programs (D+ and above), and Artisan courses. Popular options include Diploma in Business, Certificate in ICT, Diploma in Engineering, Certificate in Hospitality, and Artisan in Plumbing. Use our course checker to see all courses matching your exact grades.

How much does KUCCPS application cost?

Our course checking service costs KES 200 for your first category, and KES 100 for each additional category. This is DIFFERENT from the official KUCCPS application fee of KES 1,500 which is paid through eCitizen. Payment is via M-PESA STK Push for our service.

What are cluster points and how are they calculated?

Cluster points are your score based on your best 4 subjects in specific subject combinations required for different courses. Grade conversion: A=12, A-=11, B+=10, B=9, B-=8, C+=7, C=6, C-=5, D+=4, D=3. For example, Engineering cluster requires Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry, with typical cut-off points ranging from 36-48 depending on the university.

What are the requirements for KMTC nursing courses?

For Diploma in Nursing (KRCHN) at KMTC, you need: KCSE mean grade of C plain, C plain in English, Biology, and Chemistry, and C- in Mathematics or Physics. The program duration is 3 years with clinical training at various KMTC campuses across Kenya.

How do I apply to KUCCPS officially?

To apply to KUCCPS officially, visit students.kuccps.net. The application fee is KES 1,500 via eCitizen (M-PESA PayBill 820201). You can select up to 6 degree choices or 4 diploma/certificate choices. Applications typically open in April and close July 15th each year.

What is the minimum grade for degree programs?

Degree programs require a minimum of C+ mean grade and specific cluster points. For example, Engineering typically needs C+ in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry. Medicine requires B in Biology, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Physics.

What are the requirements for diploma programs?

Diploma programs require a minimum of C- mean grade. Most diplomas accept C plain, but some like Nursing require C plain in specific subjects. Duration is typically 2 years at technical colleges and national polytechnics.

What courses can I do with D+ in KCSE?

With a D+ mean grade, you qualify for Certificate programs (1-2 year programs) and Artisan courses. Options include Certificate in ICT, Business, Hospitality, and various technical trades.

How does the course basket feature work?

The basket lets you save and compare courses you're interested in. Click 'Add to Basket' on any course, then view your basket to see all saved courses, compare them side-by-side, and export as PDF to share with parents or counselors.

What if my M-Pesa payment fails?

If your M-Pesa payment fails, first check your balance and ensure you have sufficient funds. Verify your phone number is correct (format 07XXXXXXXX). If money was deducted but you didn't get results, use your receipt number at /verify-payment to access your results. Contact kuccpscourses@gmail.com if issues persist.
