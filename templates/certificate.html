🎓 Certificate & Short Course Programs Overview

Certificate programs are 1-2 year vocational training courses focused on practical skills development. These flexible programs prepare students for immediate employment or entrepreneurship.

  • Duration: 1-2 years (quickest path to employment)
  • Focus: Practical, job-ready skills for immediate application
  • Entry: Minimum D+ in KCSE - accessible to many students
  • Outcome: National Certificate with immediate employment potential

Certificate programs typically take 1-2 years to complete, depending on the specific course and institution. Certificate programs are general vocational courses (1-2 years, D+ entry). Craft Certificates are trade-specific qualifications (1-2 years, D entry) regulated by NITA. Both provide practical skills, but craft certificates are specifically designed for skilled trades and artisan occupations.

📋 Certificate Program Prerequisites & Requirements

Minimum Academic Requirements
  • 📊 Overall KCSE Grade: Minimum D+ - flexible entry
  • 🎓 Form 4 Completion: Completion of secondary school education
  • 📖 Entry Score: Generally flexible, low minimum requirements
  • 👤 Age Requirement: Usually 16+ years old at entry
  • ✅ Additional: Some programs may require basic numeracy and literacy
Program-Specific Requirements
  • 🍽️ Hospitality Programs: Customer service aptitude, communication skills
  • 💻 IT Programs: Basic computer literacy helpful but not required
  • 💄 Beauty Programs: Practical skills and attention to detail
  • 📈 Business Programs: Basic arithmetic and communication abilities
  • 🔧 Trade Programs: Physical ability and aptitude for hands-on work
