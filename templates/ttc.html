👨‍🏫 Teachers Training Colleges (TTC) Programs

Teacher Training Colleges (TTC) prepare passionate individuals for careers in education. These programs offer specialized training in primary teacher education, secondary subject specialization, and special needs education across Kenya's accredited training centers.

How it works: Enter your KCSE overall grade and subject grades below to find which teacher training programs you qualify for. Teaching careers are rewarding, stable, and essential for Kenya's educational development. Most TTC programs are 2-3 years in duration.

Primary teachers handle all subjects for grades 1-8 (P1 certificate). Secondary teachers specialize in 2 subjects for grades 9-12 (Diploma in Education). Secondary teaching requires higher qualifications and offers faster career progression.

TSC registration requires your teaching qualification certificate, KCSE certificate, and good conduct certificate. You must apply online through the TSC portal. Registration is mandatory for all teachers in Kenya.

Special Needs Education trains teachers to support learners with disabilities (visual, hearing, physical, intellectual). These specialized teachers are in high demand as Kenya promotes inclusive education in all schools.

TTC diplomas (2-3 years) offer faster entry to teaching with practical focus. University BEd degrees (4 years) provide deeper academic training and faster promotion to administrative roles. Both lead to TSC registration and teaching careers.

📋 Teacher Training Program Prerequisites and Requirements

Minimum Academic Requirements
  • 📊 Overall KCSE Grade: Minimum C (5 points) - higher for secondary streams
  • 📖 English Language: Minimum C for communication skills essential
  • 🔢 For Secondary: Minimum C in teaching subject required
  • 🎯 Cluster Points: Typically 40-60 points depending on TTC
  • 👤 Age Requirement: Usually 18+ years at entry
Program-Specific Requirements
  • 🏫 Primary Teaching: Love for working with children, communication skills
  • 📚 Secondary Teaching: Strong subject knowledge in chosen specialization
  • 🔧 Technical Teaching: Practical aptitude and technical subject knowledge
  • 💙 Special Needs Education: Compassion, patience, and commitment to inclusion
  • ✅ All Programs: Good character, health screening, and teaching aptitude

Comprehensive guide to KUCCPS TTC programs and teacher training courses available through the national placement system. KUCCPS TTC programs include KUCCPS primary teacher training, KUCCPS secondary teacher training, KUCCPS diploma in education, KUCCPS special needs education, KUCCPS early childhood development, and KUCCPS technical teacher training. The KUCCPS teacher training placement system allocates students to accredited Teacher Training Colleges across Kenya. KUCCPS TTC requirements vary by specialization with minimum C for diploma programs and C- for P1 certificate. KUCCPS TTC C plain holders qualify for all primary teaching and most secondary teaching programs. KUCCPS TTC C- students can access P1 teacher training and select diploma courses. KUCCPS teaching subject combinations must be approved with minimum C in both subjects for secondary teaching. KUCCPS TSC registration is required after graduation for all teaching positions. KUCCPS TTC programs 2026 include updated curricula aligned with the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC). KUCCPS teacher training colleges offer government-sponsored placements with affordable fees. KUCCPS diploma in education prepares students for TSC employment and career advancement. KUCCPS TTC fees are supported by HELB loans for eligible students. KUCCPS teaching courses remain in high demand due to teacher shortages nationwide. KUCCPS education programs provide stable government careers with pension benefits.
